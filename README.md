# Following-the-Funds-
## The Relationship Between Farm Subsidies and State Economic Indicators in Row Crop Agriculture (2015–2024)

 This repository contains the code and final research poster for an independent undergraduate research project presented at a university research gala.

## Overview

This project examines the relationship between state-level agricultural subsidy payments and key economic outcomes in row crop agriculture across the United States from 2015 to 2024. The goal is to evaluate how variation in subsidy exposure corresponds with changes in prices, land values, yields, and farm-level economic performance.

## Data

The analysis uses publicly available state-level agricultural and economic data, including subsidy payments, commodity prices, land values, agricultural yields, farm profitability measures, and broader rural economic indicators.

## Methods

Applied exploratory data analysis to a state-level panel dataset (2015–2024), including descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, and construction of derived economic indicators to evaluate relationships between subsidy exposure and economic outcomes across states and over time.

## Outputs

The repository includes the final research poster presented at a university research gala, along with the code used for data cleaning, analysis, and visualization.
