## Year

Calendar year of observation (2015-2024); 
serves as the time dimension in the state-year panel dataset.

## State

U.S State in which the agricultural observations are recorded; 
serves as the cross-sectional identifer in the state-year panel dataset.

## Soybean_Acres

Total number of acres planted with soybeans in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Soybean_Yield

Average bushels per acre of soybeans in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Total_Soybean_Production

Estimated total soybean production per state-year observation, calculated as soybean acres planted
multiplied by average soybean yield 

## Soybean_Price

Average price of soybeans in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Soybean_Revenue

Estimated revenue from soybeans per state-year observation, calculated as Production multiplied by Price

## Corn_Acres

Total number of acres planted with corn in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Corn_Yield

Average bushels per acre of corn in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Total_Corn_Production

Estimated total corn production per state-year observation, calculated as Acres multiplied by Yield

## Corn_Price

Average price of corn in a given U.S. state for a given year.

## Corn_Revenue

Estimated revenue from corn per state-year observation, calculated as Production multiplied by Price

## Subsidy

Total annual federal agricultural program payments to producers, aggregated at the state level, including commodity,
conservation, disaster, and supplemental assistance programs. Excludes crop insurance premium 
subsidies and indemnities.

## Land_Value_per_acre

USDA-reported average cropland value per acre at the state-year level, 
representing the estimated market value of land used for crop production.

## Cash_Rent_per_acre

The annual cash payment received by landowners per acre of cropland leased to operators, representing 
the rental cost of agricultural land use. Reported at the state-year level, this measure 
reflects short-run land leasing costs rather than asset values.

## Soybean_and_Corn_Revenue

The estimated total value of crop production, constructed as soybean revenue plus corn revenue. This 
measure represents gross crop output revenue prior to costs, subsidies, or insurance adjustments.
